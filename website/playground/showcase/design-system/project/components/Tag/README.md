One or two words of status beside a name. The tone means something and the word says it too, so a tag never depends on its color.

| Tone | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `neutral` | A plain fact | Whole bean |
| `new` | Roasted for the first time this season | New roast |
| `success` | Available, done, on its way | In stock |
| `seasonal` | Here for a while, then gone | Limited |
| `danger` | Stopped, and needs the person | Paused |

At most two tags per card. You provide `tone` and the word.
