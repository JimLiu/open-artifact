One coffee, enough to choose it: its label, its name in `title`, three tasting notes, the roast meter and the price. It is the unit of the shop and of the subscription picker.

- The label is the bag's own artwork, drawn from the coffee's two colors. Never a photo, never a third color.
- One tag at most, top right. The featured coffee gets the primary button; the others stay secondary, so a grid has one terracotta action.
- The roast meter is five dots, `moss` on `moss-soft`, always with the word beside it.
- The price is set in `data`: amount, then weight.
- You provide the coffee (`name`, `kicker`, `notes`, `roast`, `price`, `weight`) and `featured`.
