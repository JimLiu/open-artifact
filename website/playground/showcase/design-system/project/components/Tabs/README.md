Two ways to switch between views of the same thing. **Tabs** change what the page shows and mark the current one with an `accent` underline. A **segment** picks one of two to four options inside a form (the grind, the bag size) and stays neutral.

- Two to five tabs, one or two words each. More than five is a menu.
- The underline is the only accent in a row of tabs; labels stay `ink` and `ink-muted`.
- A count after a label is set in `data` and never colored.
- You provide `items`, the selected `value` and `onChange`.
