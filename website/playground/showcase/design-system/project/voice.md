# Voice

Juniper sounds like the person behind the counter who knows the beans and is in no hurry. Short sentences. The coffee first, then the details.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock your perfect cup! | A light roast for slow mornings. |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | We could not save your address. Check the postcode. |
| Submit | Start a subscription |
| Your order has been successfully placed | Roasting Thursday. At your door by Saturday. |

- Sentence case everywhere, buttons included. Verb first on anything you can press.
- Numbers as numerals with their unit: 250 g, 2 bags, every 2 weeks.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji. The coffee can be exciting; the interface stays calm.
- Names of coffees are capitalized and never translated: First Light, Hearth.
